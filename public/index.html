How it works

Three steps, and the second one is talking.

01

Hold

Press the button or the space bar. The dots light up when it can hear you.

02

Speak

Say it the way you would to a person. Each word shows up about half a second behind your voice, in five languages.

03

Read

The words are already there. Let go, the last ones land, the tidy-up takes out the ums, and it is ready to copy.

Tidy-up

You said it once. It should read that way.

What was heard

Um, so the the launch moves to uh Monday. we we are waiting on legal, hmm, and I will confirm by Thursday

What lands on the page

So the launch moves to Monday. We are waiting on legal, and I will confirm by Thursday.

The tidy-up is rules, not a rewrite: it removes ums and doubled words and fixes capitals. Every word left is one you said. Switch it off beside the button to see exactly what was heard.

Yours to run

Open source, on your own Gradium key.

Use your own key

Paste an API key from your Gradium account. It travels with each clip to this demo’s server, which passes it to Gradium and keeps nothing. It stays in this page only until you reload or close it.

What this demo does with your voice

The clip goes to this demo’s server and on to Gradium to be transcribed. This site keeps no audio and no text, and sets no cookies. The shared demo has a daily limit for everyone; with your own key there is none.
