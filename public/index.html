Press the button, say something, and press it again. Your words land here.

Hold the button while you speak, or tap it once to start and again to stop. Up to 30 seconds a clip.

Use your own Gradium key

Paste an API key from your Gradium account. It travels with each clip to this demo’s server, which passes it to Gradium and keeps nothing. It stays in this page only until you reload or close it.

What happens to your voice: the clip goes to this demo’s server and on to Gradium to be transcribed. This site keeps no audio and no text, and sets no cookies.
